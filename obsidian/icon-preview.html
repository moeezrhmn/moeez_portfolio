Favicon candidates

Three marks, at the sizes that matter

A favicon is judged at 16 px in a strip of twenty other tabs, never at the size it was drawn. Scroll to the tab simulations at the bottom of each row: that is the only view that decides it.

Option A

Two-tone M

Bone stems, brass chevron. The accent is structural rather than decorative, and the chevron is a large enough shape to survive downscaling. Sharp corners match a design system that has no radius anywhere.

Option B

Solid M with brass rule

Reads as a letter more immediately because it is one colour, and the rule echoes the hairlines used throughout the site. The risk is at 16 px, where that rule collapses to roughly one pixel and can look like a rendering artefact or a text underline.
